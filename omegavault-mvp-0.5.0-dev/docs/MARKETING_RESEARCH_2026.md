# OmegaVault Marketing Research Notes — September 2026

## Positioning principle

OmegaVault should be marketed as security/recovery infrastructure, not as a trading, yield, speculation or "get rich" product. The strongest initial problem statements are:

- one lost key can become a permanent single point of failure
- inheritance and continuity planning for self-custody
- recovery without handing a seed phrase to a company
- chain-aware recovery rather than a one-chain wallet story
- transparent, testable recovery rules

Current security reporting supports a security-first market narrative: wallet compromise and targeted social engineering remain major loss categories in 2026. Advertising copy should cite credible sources rather than use fear-heavy claims.

## Paid-channel reality

### Google

Google's current crypto policy is restrictive. It explicitly lists unhosted software wallets among prohibited examples, while other cryptocurrency software-wallet advertising is restricted/certification-dependent by jurisdiction. Do not plan the first paid acquisition engine around Google Search until the final product classification and advertiser eligibility have been reviewed.

### TikTok

TikTok's current financial-services policy explicitly lists non-custodial wallets as prohibited in some market rules. Treat TikTok paid advertising as a later compliance review, not the launch channel. Organic educational short-form content is a separate question from paid eligibility.

### X

X allows cryptocurrency-wallet advertising only with restrictions and country-specific certification/licensing requirements. X remains valuable organically because crypto discourse is concentrated there, but paid traffic quality must be measured carefully. Recent community reports describe bot-heavy paid traffic for some crypto-app campaigns, so clicks alone should never be the success metric.

### Reddit

Reddit is the clearest paid-media test candidate once legal/platform eligibility is confirmed. Reddit supports community, keyword, interest, retargeting and conversation placements. Its own crypto/finance case studies show that security-focused and education-oriented creative can work with crypto audiences. Start with narrow community/context targeting rather than broad reach.

## Organic / earned channels before large ad spend

1. X: build-in-public security engineering, testnet proof threads, threat-model diagrams, recovery demonstrations.
2. Reddit: participate transparently in wallet/security/inheritance discussions; do not astroturf or spam. Paid ads should read like useful Reddit posts, not generic banners.
3. YouTube: long-form demonstrations ("what happens if a key is lost?") plus short clips showing a recovery flow on testnet.
4. Podcasts/newsletters: crypto security, self-custody, estate planning, fintech and developer audiences.
5. Security/developer communities: GitHub, Ethereum/XRPL/Solana/Bitcoin developer communities, hackathons and security conferences.
6. Partnerships: hardware-wallet educators, estate-planning professionals familiar with digital assets, security researchers, wallet providers, family-office/RIAs only after legal/compliance positioning is ready.
7. Product proof: public testnet deployments, audit reports, source fingerprints, bug bounty, security documentation. For a recovery product, proof is marketing.

## Timing starting points

Use platform timing as a starting hypothesis, not a permanent rule.

- X: 2026 Sprout Social data shows strongest general engagement Tue-Thu roughly 12-6 p.m. local time; its financial-services data leans Tue-Fri workday hours.
- LinkedIn: financial-services engagement is strongest Tue-Wed roughly 10 a.m.-3 p.m. local time, with weekday business-hour strength.
- Instagram: Buffer's 2026 analysis finds strong Wednesday/Thursday performance and generally strong evening engagement; use primarily for simple consumer education, not technical conversion.
- YouTube long-form: Buffer's 2026 data points to evening windows, especially Sun-Tue; Shorts often perform strongly late week/evenings.
- Reddit paid: do not aggressively daypart at launch. Run an always-on control first, measure conversion quality by hour/day, then test scheduling only after enough data exists. Reddit supports dayparting, but community reports in 2026 describe budget-pacing problems when small campaigns are compressed into narrow windows.

All times should be treated in the target audience's local time. Once OmegaVault has its own data, first-party conversion timing overrides generic benchmarks.

## Initial ad creative concepts

### "The seed phrase paradox"

Hook: Self-custody gives you control. It also gives you a single point of failure.

Demonstration: show an owner heartbeat, trusted-contact threshold and rescue destination on testnet.

CTA: See how non-custodial recovery works.

### "Recovery without custody"

Hook: A recovery system should not need your seed phrase.

Creative: animation showing the seed/private key staying in the wallet while public policy state and signatures move through OmegaVault.

CTA: Test the recovery flow.

### "Inheritance stress test"

Hook: If your family had to recover your crypto tomorrow, could they?

Creative: neutral continuity checklist, not fear-heavy copy.

CTA: Build a recovery policy.

### "Multi-chain is not EVM with different logos"

Developer-facing creative showing Ethereum, XRPL, Solana and Bitcoin using different connector models behind the same Core policy.

CTA: Read the architecture / join the test program.

## Measurement

Do not optimize to impressions or clicks. Primary funnel events should be:

1. qualified landing-page session
2. architecture/security page viewed
3. demo/test flow started
4. wallet connected
5. policy created
6. test deployment completed
7. recovery test completed
8. return visit / second session
9. paid subscription when commercial launch begins

Track acquisition source through each stage. A channel with expensive clicks but high recovery-test completion can be more valuable than cheap bot-heavy traffic.

## Launch experiment structure

Phase 0 — credibility:
- public architecture
- testnet proof
- audit roadmap
- security FAQ
- founder/product story

Phase 1 — organic signal:
- 3-4 weeks of X/Reddit/YouTube education
- record which messages generate qualified questions and demo starts

Phase 2 — small paid tests:
- Reddit first if eligibility confirmed
- tightly targeted placements
- 3 creative concepts x 2 landing-page variants
- use conversion events deeper than click

Phase 3 — retargeting:
- people who read security docs or started the demo but did not complete it

Phase 4 — partnerships:
- security creators, wallet educators, estate-planning/digital-asset professionals
- compensate for content/distribution transparently; require disclosure
